04The cuts

Three tests that matter more than the total

Broken link

Any core stage below 2 caps the practical loop.

pliability — capped at re-entry (1).

Bend — capped twice, diagnosis (1) and progression (1). It can't start the loop or close it.

GOWOD — nothing below 2. The only intact chain of the three.

Causality

Can it explain why an input changed a recommendation?

pliability — and on the re-test it does what this cut actually asks: attributes a measured change to the work. Back/Core 53→65. The only causal claim about a body in the study.

GOWOD — shows its working: the protocol lists the time, equipment and workout it was built from.

Bend — per exercise, beautifully, for an input it immediately forgets.

Graduation

Capability and insight gained, or usage accumulated?

pliability — the only split verdict, and it falls on routing. The completion ceremony reports 5h 1m, 21 routines, 53 poses and a quote. Run the re-test yourself and you get 66→73 and a retired compensation pattern. The insight exists; the ceremony doesn't contain it.

Nobody graduates. On pliability's own screen, Most improved and Needs attention name the same area. Nothing closes, so no new goal opens.

GOWOD and Bend — usage only.

Friction, and the zero that flatters

Scored 0–3 per stage. One result matters more than the ledger: Bend scores zero friction on measurement because Bend has no measurement. An absent stage costs nothing, so absence reads as efficiency. Friction should only be scored within stages that exist — a note back to the rubric, not just the apps.

05Read

Everything is built. Almost nothing routes.

The headline changed in the last week of the study, and it changed in the apps' favor.

I started certain this category measures a body once and never checks. Wrong. Run pliability's test a second time and it produces a scored trajectory (66→73), a per-area delta with a tooltip (Back/Core 53→65), a plain-language claim, a dropped compensation pattern, and an offer to build the next program from the new numbers.

Measured, re-measured, delta rendered, next plan offered. The whole join is built.

And nothing routes a member to it. No prompt fired. The completion ceremony, ten days from the due date, never mentioned it. I went and got it. The most valuable screen in the category sits behind a door nobody knocks on. GOWOD holds the other half: it pushed me to re-test, the only notification in six weeks aimed at an instrument. Better reminder, weaker instrument. Between them they hold a complete product.

What's still genuinely absent is graduation — and underneath that progress screen, "we currently don't have any recommended videos that match your needs." An empty state at the exact moment the product knows the most it has ever known about this body.

Why the scores still sit where they do

Bend scores lowest and has the best engagement trend and the largest audience by an order of magnitude. Not a contradiction: these dimensions measure whether a product changes a user's state and makes it legible, not whether it can acquire users cheaply. Bend earns $4.54 per monthly active user. GOWOD and pliability earn $58 to $86.

This is a pricing-power instrument, not an engagement one.

So the opportunity is smaller units, not more features

The library, the camera, per-side measurement, AI rationale and a working re-test all already ship. The missing layer is small, frequent units of engagement that make a member feel attended to, each producing or spending a piece of state: a five-second check-in scoped to the diagnosed area, a fifteen-second single-movement spot-check, a one-question debrief, a weekly adjustment that cites last week, a notification that names the area rather than the streak.

The test for whether a unit earns its place: does it produce data or spend it? A unit that does neither is content.

Participation mechanics move frequency. Verification mechanics move price. Micro-units are how verification gets a cadence.

One tester, six weeks. Sharp for finding mechanisms, blunt for sizing them. No app is penalized here for something I didn't test, or credited for a dimension left out because it would have scored badly.
